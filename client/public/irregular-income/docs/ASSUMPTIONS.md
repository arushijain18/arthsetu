# Assumptions and items to verify before public rollout

## Product assumptions

- The user chooses the income pattern that best matches how money usually arrives. Some people have mixed income; the pattern field in My Plan can be edited to say “mixed”.
- The Floor Number uses one month as its time period and includes housing/rent, ration and utilities, school and essential health, and necessary work costs, as in the supplied guide.
- The weak-month income field is optional. A person can continue without forecasting income.
- The examples are teaching illustrations only. They are not an income target, savings target, recommended allocation or guarantee.
- The simulator accepts any rupee amount. It does not force a split or recommend a percentage.
- Tier 3 shows four money jobs—tax reserve, business, personal draw and buffer—because the guide's written instruction says to split every payment into those four jobs, while the visual three-step combines “personal + buffer”.
- Browser text-to-speech is used rather than pre-recorded audio. Available voices depend on the device and operating system.
- Local browser storage is not encrypted and is not suitable for sensitive records. A shared-device user should use fictional values or clear their saved data after use.

## Content and legal checks

- Government scheme rules, premiums, eligibility, local delivery and application processes change. The directory was checked on **24 November 2026** against official pages, but this date does not guarantee that every local office has the same process. Verify again before a public programme.
- e-Shram is described as a free worker registration/service gateway; it is not described as an automatic cash benefit.
- PM-SYM is contributory and eligibility restrictions apply. Contributions and age-based terms must be checked before enrolment.
- PMSBY and PMJJBY are treated as renewable insurance cover. Current premium, exclusions, claim terms and account conditions must be checked before joining.
- PM-JAY eligibility for most families is list-based. The 70+ senior expansion has separate terms; users should confirm the current beneficiary and card process.
- Rural employment information is labelled using the VB–G RAM G Act, 2025 and its effective date of 1 July 2026, based on the official India Code and PIB information checked for this build. Check the current state process at the Gram Panchayat or Block office.
- PM SVANidhi is described as a working-capital loan, not a grant. Local vendor identification, loan tranches and repayment rules must be checked on the official portal/Urban Local Body.
- NFSA ration entitlements depend on the applicable state or union-territory rules and beneficiary status.
- Tax treatment depends on each person's facts and the law for the relevant financial year/assessment year. The module gives record-keeping prompts but does not calculate personal tax or a universal tax reserve.

## Must be verified by the project owner

1. Ask local workers in the intended districts whether the names and pictures used for daily, weekly and project work feel representative.
2. Verify all linked schemes, current eligibility, application steps and helpline numbers with the relevant State departments before publishing a printed version.
3. Have a qualified tax professional review the Tier 3 tax module for the relevant financial year and business structure.
4. Conduct low-literacy usability tests in both languages, including users who share a phone and users with limited mobile data.
5. Review image permissions/licences before commercial or public deployment. Uploaded images have been used as supplied for this prototype; this build does not grant third-party rights to the project owner.
6. Decide who is responsible for maintaining and date-stamping `content/schemes.json` after launch.
